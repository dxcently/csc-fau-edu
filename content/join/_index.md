## What membership is

A club account gives you the club's own services. These are the practice range, the club code repos, and a private network (VPN, coming soon) that reaches them. The wiki stays public. You do not need an account to read it.

An account is free. It has nothing to do with your FAU student account or your grades.

## Who can join

FAU students come first. Students from other schools are welcome too. Everyone is approved by an officer, with no exceptions.

Use your school email when you sign up. The club uses it to check you are a student. Read what the club keeps about you in the [privacy notice](/join/privacy/) before you sign up.

## What happens after you sign up

1. Open **Sign up**. Choose a username. Enter your name and your school email.
2. Your account is **pending**. You cannot sign in to any club service yet.
3. An officer reviews the request. This is a person, so it can take a few days.
4. Once approved, **Sign in** works. Open the [portal](/portal/) to see what you can use.

If nothing happens after a week, ask in the [club Discord](http://discord.gg/2Yun8WAUuy).

## What you get

| Level | What you can use |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| Member | The practice range. The club code repos on Gitea. The VPN to the member ranges (coming soon). |
| Officer | Everything a member has. Also the club dashboard, the range controls, and the approval queue. |

The practice range is a set of machines you are allowed to attack. Nowhere else is that allowed. Read [Club Lab](/lab/) for the rules.

## Rules

You are an FAU student, or a student of your school, before you are a club member. School rules still apply to you. Do not attack anything outside the range. Do not share your account.

This is the cybersecurity club of a student organization. It is not an FAU department and does not speak for the university.
