A club account gives you the club's own services: the practice range, the club code repos, and a private network (VPN, coming soon) that reaches them. It is free, it has nothing to do with your FAU student account, and an officer approves every request. You do not need an account to read the wiki.

Sign up with your school email. Your account stays pending until an officer approves it, usually within a few days.

This is a student organization. It is not an FAU department and does not speak for the university.
