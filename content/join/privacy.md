This is the privacy notice for the club's sign-up. It covers the club accounts only. It is short on purpose. Read all of it before you sign up.

## What the club collects

- Your **username**.
- Your **display name**.
- Your **email address**.
- Your **school name and state**. The club looks these up from the domain of your email address. For example, the part after the `@`. It uses the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard API, or the Hipo university list. Only the domain goes to these lookups. Your full address does not.
- **Sign-in records**: when you signed in, from which network address, and whether it worked.

## Why

- Username, display name and email identify your account and let officers contact you.
- School name and state let an officer confirm you are a student, and tell the club which schools its members come from.
- Sign-in records help officers find abuse and fix problems.

The club does not use this data for advertising. It does not sell it.

## Where it is stored

On servers the club runs itself. It is not stored on FAU systems.

## Who sees it

Club officers. Other members cannot see your email address or your sign-in records.

The club does not share this data with FAU or with anyone else. The exception is a legal duty to do so. If you break the club's rules on the range, officers may pass the relevant records to the school.

## How long the club keeps it

Until you ask for deletion, or until you leave the club. Sign-in records are kept for as long as the account exists.

## Ask for deletion

Email [faucyberclub@gmail.com](mailto:faucyberclub@gmail.com) from the address on your account. Say you want your account and data deleted. An officer deletes it and confirms by email.

Deletion removes your club account. It does not change anything in your school records, because the club has no access to them.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new text goes on this page. The club is a student organization run by volunteers. Ask an officer if something here is unclear.
